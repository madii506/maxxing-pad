FAQ

Everything about maxxing: fees, the ladder, stonk pairs, launching, trading and $MAXXING.

Basics

What is maxxing?

A Solana launchpad that maxxes everything pump.fun stopped maxxing. Fees are minned to 0.5% a trade instead of 1.25%. Creators get 0.3% of every trade. Snipers who buy in block 0 pay 99%. And every coin climbs a ladder from Mid to Ascended, earned by real buyers, not bots.

Is maxxing live? Can I launch a coin today?

The site is live; launching isn't yet. Launches open with $MAXXING. Until then you can browse the live board of StonkFun and pump.fun coins with their maxx levels, see what stonks StonkFun coins are paired with, open any coin's chart and stats, trade through Jupiter in your own wallet, keep a watchlist, run the maxx check and check whether a ticker is taken.

Why "maxxing"?

Everyone on CT is maxxing something. Looksmaxxing, auramaxxing, rizzmaxxing. pump.fun stopped: same 1.25% on every trade, snipers still own block 0, and a coin is either pumping or dead. maxxing is the launchpad that never stops. Your coin isn't mid. It's just early.

Which chain is maxxing on?

Solana.

Is maxxing part of pump.fun, StonkFun or Jupiter?

No. maxxing is independent. The board shows StonkFun and pump.fun coins so you can compare, charts come from DEX Screener, and trades run through Jupiter.

Fees

How much does a trade cost on maxxing?

0.5% of every trade, buys and sells, after the first block.

How does that compare to pump.fun?

pump.fun charges 1.25% on its bonding curve: 0.95% to the platform and 0.30% to the creator, per BloFin's fee guide from September 2026. A $1,000 buy costs $12.50 in fees on pump.fun and $5.00 on maxxing.

Where does the 0.5% go?

0.3% of every trade goes to the coin's creator. 0.2% buys back $MAXXING.

What is the block 0 fee?

The fee starts at 99% in the first block of a launch and falls to 0.5% within seconds. Snipers and bundles that buy in the launch block pay it. Everyone after them pays 0.5%. You can see the curve on the launch page.

Do creators earn more on maxxing?

The creator's cut matches pump.fun's: 0.30% of every trade. The difference is on the trader's side, who pays 0.5% instead of 1.25%.

What do StonkFun coins charge?

Each StonkFun coin sets its own transfer fee, and its coin page shows it.

How are the fee numbers on the site worked out?

Each coin's last-hour volume from Jupiter, multiplied by the fee: 1.25% for pump.fun's bonding curve, the reported transfer fee for StonkFun coins, and 0.5% for maxxing. Graduated coins can pay a different rate, so treat them as estimates.

What is the maxx check?

A calculator for your own trading. Set your average trade size and how many trades you make a day, and it shows what pump.fun's 1.25% costs you in a year against maxxing's 0.5%, at the live SOL price.

The ladder

What is the maxx ladder?

Every coin has a level: Mid, Maxxing, Mogging or Ascended. The level comes from the coin's maxx score, which is Jupiter's organic score: how much of its trading looks like real people rather than bots. Real buyers move a coin up. Bots don't.

What are the levels?

Mid is a maxx score under 40. Maxxing is 40 to 69. Mogging is 70 to 89. Ascended is 90 and up. Coins Jupiter hasn't scored yet show as Unrated.

Does the ladder use real data?

Yes. Every level on the board is worked out live from Jupiter's public token data. Nothing is simulated, and the counts on the ladder are the coins on the board right now.

What will levels do for maxxing coins?

On maxxing, a coin's level is its rank on the board: higher levels get the top spots, the glow and the Ascended tab. Levels are earned by real buyers, so nobody can buy their way up with bots.

Launching

How will launching work?

Name, ticker, image, the asset you pair with, a description and your socials. The coin goes live on a bonding curve with maxxing's fees built in: 99% in block 0, then 0.5% a trade, 0.3% of it to you. It starts at Mid and climbs from there. The launch page already shows exactly how your card will look on the board.

When do launches open?

With $MAXXING. Follow maxxing on 𝕏 for the date.

What does "pair with" mean?

The asset your coin's curve is priced in, the way StonkFun coins pair with tokenized stocks and other assets. The launch page lists what StonkFun coins are paired with today.

What does the ticker check do?

Type a ticker on the launch page and maxxing searches StonkFun and pump.fun (through Jupiter) for coins with that exact ticker, so you know before you launch whether it's taken.

The board

Where do the coins on the board come from?

StonkFun coins come from StonkFun's public API, and pump.fun coins from Jupiter's public token data, with Jupiter adding holders, volume and the maxx score. Charts come from DEX Screener. None of them are maxxing coins.

What are stonk pairs?

StonkFun coins are priced against a quote asset, often a tokenized stock. Stonk pairs counts what the StonkFun coins on the board are paired with right now. Tap one to see only those coins.

How often does it update?

New coins every 8 to 10 seconds, Trending every 30 seconds, maxx scores every 45 seconds, Top market cap every minute. Market caps flash green or red when they change.

What do the tabs mean?

New is the latest launches. Trending is Jupiter's top trending coins over the last hour. Ascended and Mogging show the coins at those levels. Movers is the biggest moves over the last 5 minutes. Top mcap is the largest market caps. Watchlist is the coins you starred.

Why does a coin show letters instead of its logo?

That's rare. maxxing tries pump.fun's image server, the coin's own image, Jupiter and the coin's on-chain metadata, in that order, and tries again 12 seconds later for brand-new coins.

Trading & safety

How do I trade a coin?

Open any coin and use the trade box. Swaps run through Jupiter and you sign them in your own wallet. maxxing never holds your funds and never sees your keys.

Which wallets work?

Phantom, Solflare and Backpack.

Does maxxing add a fee when I trade board coins?

No. You pay that coin's own platform fee (StonkFun's or pump.fun's) and Solana's network fee. maxxing's 0.5% applies to coins launched on maxxing.

What should I check before I buy?

Every coin page shows its maxx level, whether mint and freeze authority are switched off, what share the top 10 holders own, buys against sells over the last hour, liquidity, holders and the dev wallet. Memecoins are high risk and can go to zero. Nothing on maxxing is financial advice.

Where is my watchlist saved?

Only in your browser. It never leaves your device.
